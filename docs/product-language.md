# Product and financial language

Player-facing copy uses Sparks and qualified language such as “up to” and “may receive”.

Owner-facing copy uses:

- Campaign balance
- Promotional balance
- Campaign credits
- Campaign budget
- Campaign usage

The product must not describe player participation as play-to-earn, passive income, an investment,
crypto, easy money, or licensing. Players never see owner bids, owner payment allocation, Nortix
margin, subsidy, fraud scores, or private accounting calculations.

Sparks are non-withdrawable, non-transferable, have no cash value, and cannot be exchanged for
money. They fund cosmetics, non-financial progression, and eligible Nortix-sponsored gift requests.

Private server-owner store reporting may show delivery-confirmed estimated proceeds in a supported
currency. Never describe this as a player-facing Sparks conversion or cash equivalent. Use
qualified language such as “may be eligible,” “estimated,” “subject to review,” and “provider
requirements may apply.” These figures must never be serialized to player store, purchase,
identity, profile, or activity responses.
Sponsored gift copy must make clear that Nortix Labs independently supplies the gift and is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with the named third party. Reputation is separate
again: it cannot be purchased and reflects useful, honest participation.
